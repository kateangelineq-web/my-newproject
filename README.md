ShopSure: An AI-Powered Online Shopping Trust Checker 

Building AI course project 

## Summary

ShopSure is an AI-powered tool that helps online shoppers evaluate the trustworthiness of online sellers and product listings. It analyzes seller ratings, customer reviews, product information, and other available details to help consumers make more informed purchasing decisions.

## Background

Online shopping has become a convenient way for people to purchase products. However, not all online sellers are trustworthy. Some consumers experience problems such as misleading product descriptions, fake reviews, poor-quality products, and unreliable sellers.

ShopSure aims to help address these problems by using Artificial Intelligence to analyze available information and provide consumers with an idea of how trustworthy an online seller or product listing may be.

The main problems it aims to address are:

* Difficulty identifying trustworthy online sellers.
* Misleading product descriptions and information.
* Fake or suspicious customer reviews.
* Lack of confidence when purchasing from unfamiliar online shops.

I chose this idea because online shopping is part of everyday life, and consumers deserve to feel more confident when making purchasing decisions. As a Marketing Management student, I am also interested in understanding how trust and credibility influence consumer behavior.

## How is it used?

ShopSure can be used by online shoppers before making a purchase, especially when buying from unfamiliar sellers or platforms.

The process works as follows:

1. The user enters or provides available information about an online seller or product listing.
2. The AI system analyzes details such as seller ratings, customer reviews, product descriptions, and other available indicators.
3. The system identifies possible warning signs and positive trust indicators.
4. ShopSure provides an assessment to help users understand whether the listing appears trustworthy or needs further checking.
5. The user can consider the results before deciding to purchase.

The main users of this tool are online shoppers who want to make safer and more informed purchasing decisions.

## Data Sources and AI Methods

ShopSure can use publicly available information and sample datasets about online shopping, customer reviews, seller ratings, and product listings.

Possible data sources include:

* Publicly available product and seller information from online shopping platforms, subject to their terms and permissions.
* Sample datasets containing customer reviews and ratings.
* User-provided product and seller information.

The project may use the following AI methods:

* **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** – to analyze customer reviews and identify positive, negative, or suspicious language patterns.
* **Sentiment Analysis** – to understand the general opinions and experiences expressed in customer reviews.
* **Machine Learning Classification** – to categorize product listings or sellers based on available trust indicators.
* **Data Analysis** – to examine patterns in seller ratings, reviews, and product information.

These methods can help the system identify patterns that may indicate whether a seller or product listing deserves further checking.

## Challenges

ShopSure has some limitations that should be considered.

First, AI cannot guarantee that an online seller is completely trustworthy or fraudulent. Its assessment will depend on the quality and accuracy of the available data.

Second, customer reviews may be biased, fake, or incomplete. Some trustworthy sellers may also receive negative reviews because of isolated customer experiences.

Privacy is another important consideration. The system should only use information that can legally and ethically be accessed.

Lastly, ShopSure should serve as a guide rather than the final basis for purchasing decisions. Consumers should still use their own judgment when shopping online.

## What Next?

In the future, ShopSure could be improved by supporting more online shopping platforms and analyzing additional information about sellers and products.

The project could also include a simple website or mobile application where users can check product listings more easily.

To develop this project further, I would need additional knowledge of Python programming, machine learning, data analysis, and web development. I would also benefit from guidance from AI developers and access to reliable datasets.

My goal is to develop ShopSure into a useful tool that promotes consumer awareness, improves online shopping confidence, and encourages safer purchasing decisions.
